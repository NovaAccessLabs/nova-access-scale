# Privacy Policy — NovaAccessScale

_Last updated: 9 July 2026_

NovaAccessScale ("the app") is developed by NovaAccessLabs. This policy explains
what the app does and does not do with your information.

## Summary

NovaAccessScale does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no
user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

## Data the app handles

- **Bluetooth**: The app uses Bluetooth to scan for and connect to your kitchen
  scale and to read the weight it reports. This communication stays between your
  phone and your scale. The Bluetooth scanning permission is declared with the
  `neverForLocation` flag — the app does not use Bluetooth to derive your
  location.
- **Your settings and presets**: Preferences (feedback options, high contrast)
  and the target-weight presets you create are stored only on your device, using
  the Android system's local storage. They are never uploaded anywhere.
- **Speech**: Spoken announcements are produced on-device by your phone's
  text-to-speech engine.

## Data we collect

None. The app does not send any data off your device. It has no servers and makes
no network requests to us.

## Permissions

- **Bluetooth (scan / connect)** — to find and talk to your scale.
- **Vibrate** — to provide haptic feedback.

## Third parties

The app does not share data with third parties. Links you choose to open (such as
our email or Instagram) hand off to the relevant app on your device; anything you
do there is governed by that service's own privacy policy.

## Children

The app is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children.
